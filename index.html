Welcome to Spirito Royal Astana

Founded in the vibrant heart of Astana, Spirito Royal represents more than a conventional haircutting salon; it is an authentic men's club dedicated to timeless styling traditions and uncompromising craftsmanship. Our philosophy balances classical British barbering discipline with modern European trends. Guests visit our locations not only for precise scissor work and immaculate fades, but also to disconnect from the frantic daily rhythm of Kazakhstan's capital while enjoying freshly roasted single-origin espresso.

Every workstation inside our lounges features ergonomic Japanese chairs, premium Italian cosmetic lines, and strictly sanitized instruments. Our resident barbers take pride in tailoring each contour to the individual skeletal geometry of the client, guaranteeing a confident profile that retains its pristine shape for weeks. We honor appointments punctually and build relationships anchored in mutual trust and genuine hospitality.

Philosophy of Our Craft
