Join Our Team and Academy

Due to the steady expansion of our network in Astana, Spirito Royal consistently seeks dedicated professionals eager to refine their skills in an environment of excellence. We offer transparent career paths spanning junior apprenticeships to senior brand directorships and coaching positions within our academy. Our specialists benefit from premium Japanese ergonomics, continuous customer acquisition pipelines, and timely remuneration structures.

For candidates without previous salon background, our academy hosts comprehensive foundation courses. The program balances rigorous ergonomic scissor drills and live model workshops under the direct mentorship of capital masters with theoretical training in hair anatomy and client etiquette.
